About MyTourist

We connect curious travelers with unforgettable experiences — thoughtful itineraries, trusted local guides, and handpicked stays. Our mission is simple: make travel meaningful, accessible, and responsible.

Created as a college mini project, MyTourist is a student-built platform that blends creativity, technology, and a shared passion for travel. It represents our teamwork, innovation, and dedication to learning real-world web development.

How We Built It

MyTourist was developed as part of our B.Tech coursework using modern web technologies and strong collaboration. Our focus was on user experience, responsive design, and simplicity.

  • ✔ Developed using Django (Python) and Bootstrap.
  • ✔ Designed for all screen sizes with a clean, minimal interface.
  • ✔ Created with teamwork, learning, and innovation in mind.
